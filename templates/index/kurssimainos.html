Interested in judo?

Join the Judo Kickstart intensive course!

JURRI is hosting a weekend-long judo camp in early February in Bommari (Hervanta Campus). It consists of four judo sessions and one slideshow presentation and it is aimed for complete beginners*. The course will go over the core basics of judo training and judo as a sport as well as JURRI as a club and the people in it. This intensive course offers great basis for the regular JURRI sessions and works as a kickstart to a new hobby.
